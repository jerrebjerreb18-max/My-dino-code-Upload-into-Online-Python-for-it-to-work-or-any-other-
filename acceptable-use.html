Use the tools responsibly

Acceptable Use Policy

These rules explain how to use Potential Millionaires websites, Discord tools, and dashboards safely and with permission.

Last updated: 14 November 2026

1. Use only what you are authorized to use

Use the dashboard only for a Discord server and ER:LC server where you have permission. Server owners are responsible for choosing staff roles and deciding who may use moderation or game-server actions. Do not share access codes, tokens, or credentials, or use someone else’s account or server without permission.

2. Do not misuse the service

  • Do not break laws, Discord’s rules, Roblox’s rules, or ER:LC’s rules.
  • Do not attempt to bypass sign-in, role checks, rate limits, security controls, or server-owner decisions.
  • Do not use the bot or dashboard to harass, threaten, scam, impersonate, spam, raid, or disrupt a person or community.
  • Do not probe, damage, overload, scrape, or interfere with our systems or a third-party service.
  • Do not submit content or files that you have no right to share, or that expose another person’s private information.
  • Do not use ER:LC commands to act on a player unless you have permission and a legitimate server reason.

3. Respect and review

Automated filters and AI-assisted features can make mistakes. They are not proof that someone broke a rule. Human server staff remain responsible for reviewing reports and deciding what action, if any, is appropriate. Do not use an automated result alone to punish someone.

4. Reports and enforcement

Tell the relevant server staff about suspected misuse through a private support channel. We or a server owner may suspend access to protect people, a server, or the service, subject to applicable law and any rights you have to challenge a decision.

5. Related policies

This policy forms part of the Terms of Service. Information about data handled by the tools is in the Privacy Policy.

6. Contact

Contact Potential Millionaires by emailing potentialmillionairesofficial@gmail.com or through the Discord support server, where you can open a private support ticket.
