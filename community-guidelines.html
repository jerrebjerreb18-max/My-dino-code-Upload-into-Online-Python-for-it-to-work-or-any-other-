Community Guidelines

These guidelines apply in Potential Millionaires community spaces and when using our community tools.

Last updated: 14 November 2026

1. Treat people with respect

Be civil, even when you disagree. Harassment, hate, threats, targeted insults, sexual harassment, and repeated unwanted contact are not acceptable.

2. Keep the community safe

Do not scam, phish, impersonate, share malicious links, organize raids, or encourage others to disrupt a server. Do not post another person’s private information, credentials, or private messages without permission.

3. Post in the right place

Follow channel instructions, avoid flooding or repeated self-promotion, and use private support channels for account, application, or moderation issues. Do not post sensitive information in public channels or in AI review prompts.

4. Use reports and moderation tools responsibly

Make reports honestly and include relevant context. Do not use server commands to retaliate or target someone. Automated filters and AI suggestions can be wrong; staff should review the available evidence before making decisions.

5. Staff decisions and appeals

Follow reasonable moderator instructions. If you believe a moderation action was mistaken, ask for review through the server’s appeal or support process rather than escalating publicly or repeatedly contacting staff.

6. Enforcement

Server staff may remove content or limit access according to the server’s rules. Serious or repeated conduct may lead to removal from a community. These guidelines do not replace Discord, Roblox, ER:LC, or other providers’ rules.

7. Contact

For help, email potentialmillionairesofficial@gmail.com or contact Potential Millionaires through the Discord support server and open a private support ticket.
