28 November 2026

CommunityDesk Support Update

The Support guide now covers private status checks for reports and staff applications. These commands become available once the bot update is installed.

Staff can use the new ticket queue tools for assignments, priorities, and handoff notes, and the desk commands for backlogs and setup checks. Members with The Potentials role or Administrator permission can preview and confirm closed-ticket cleanup.

26 November 2026

OpenAI support and new staff tools

This Millionaire Bot release switched support replies to OpenAI and added more ways for verified staff to work from Discord.

  • Tickets — Rename a support ticket by mentioning the bot and giving it a new name.
  • Announcements — Reply to a message and ask the bot to repost it under the bot’s name.
  • Staff tools — Create slash commands with saved text replies and send polished follow-up DMs to members who opted in.

If you had automatic AI replies on in a support ticket, select Toggle AI replies to opt in again with OpenAI.

In Potential Millionaires, directly asking the bot to run a staff-only action without access can trigger a one-hour timeout. Members can still ask questions and get support.

Find bot commands and help on the Discord support page. Read the updated Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
