Your information

Privacy Policy

This notice explains what Potential Millionaires may process when you visit the website or use our Discord tools and ER:LC dashboard, why we use it, and how to contact us about your data.

Last updated: 26 November 2026

1. Who operates the services

Data controller: Jeroen Buelens, operating the Potential Millionaires project.

Privacy contact: Email potentialmillionairesofficial@gmail.com, or open a private support ticket in our Discord support server. Please do not post private information in a public channel.

2. Information we may process and store

  • Website visits: our pages do not use advertising or analytics trackers. GitHub Pages, as the host, may process technical request information such as IP address, browser details, requested page, and request time to deliver and secure the site.
  • Bot protection: when you open the staff/ER:LC dashboard entry screen, sign in, or submit a moderator code, Cloudflare Turnstile processes client-side signals such as your IP address, TLS fingerprint, browser User-Agent, the widget site key, and the site origin to check for automated abuse. We receive a verification result, not a profile of those signals, and do not store the completed challenge token or those signals in our database. Cloudflare handles its own processing and retention under its Turnstile Privacy Addendum.
  • Discord bot database: the bot stores server configuration and role permissions; Discord server, channel, role, and user IDs; ticket/case owner, claimant, room, and status; ticket AI settings; reported account IDs, evidence message references and staff moderation decisions; staff-application status, progress, and answers while an application is in progress; shift start/end times; leveling XP and timestamps; role rewards and self-role settings; and rate-limit or anti-spam counters. The current bot code writes these records to a local SQLite database file on the machine where the bot runs.
  • Tickets, applications, moderation, and audit records: ticket transcripts saved to a server archive can contain message dates, author names and Discord IDs, message IDs and text, embed text, attachment names, and attachment URLs. Report videos submitted with /report-evidence may be copied to the server’s configured private staff archive or log channel for authorized staff to review. Evidence submitted through the report and partnership ticket flows stays in Discord. Application answers and moderation or anti-raid reports may also be posted in channels configured by the server owner. ER:LC action logs may include the target Roblox user ID, action, reason, moderator name and Discord ID, and result. Discord channel records are controlled by that server’s settings and staff.
  • Dashboard sign-in and access: Discord user ID and display name, server membership and current role IDs, and moderator access requests and approvals. The Discord OAuth access token is used briefly to fetch your Discord identity and is not retained. A short-lived dashboard session token is held in your browser session storage; Cloudflare D1 stores a hash of it. OAuth state and join-code values are also stored as hashes, alongside short-term join-code attempt counters. Pending or approved moderator records include Discord ID, display name, and request/approval time.
  • ER:LC connection and server data: the dashboard’s private-server API key is encrypted and stored in Cloudflare D1. The Discord bot version stores a connected server’s key in its local environment file on the machine where the bot runs; the bot does not write that key to its Discord logs. When requested, the tools retrieve the server name and player counts, player names and Roblox IDs, teams/permissions, and ban-list information from ER:LC. The dashboard does not store a separate ER:LC server ID or player roster in D1. The bot host may have separate machine or backup records.
  • Commands: dashboard commands process the target Roblox user ID, action, reason, and staff member identity. The dashboard posts approval, completion, or failure audit messages to the configured Discord staff-log channel; the dashboard does not keep a separate command-history table in D1.
  • Automatic AI support: when you enable OpenAI replies in a routine private support ticket, the bot can reply without a mention. It may send OpenAI the grouped support messages it is answering and up to 10 recent earlier messages you wrote after switching it on, along with relevant project information. This mode uses text only. You can turn it off from the ticket at any time and continue with staff support. Earlier Groq opt-ins do not enable OpenAI replies.
  • AI help by mention: when you mention the bot, it may send OpenAI your request and relevant project information. Outside a private ticket, it may also send images or sampled frames from up to three attached files. When it processes a video, it sends up to three sampled frames from that video. Keep passwords, payment details, government ID numbers, and other sensitive information out of AI requests.
  • Public web lookup: when you ask the bot to search the web in a public Potential Millionaires channel, it sends your search question to OpenAI to find public sources. Keep the question general and leave personal details out of it. The bot posts the answer and source links in that channel.
  • Staff requests and drafts: OpenAI may interpret the text of a staff member’s explicit bot mention. The bot verifies Discord permissions before running any action. If the rewrite option is enabled, OpenAI may copy-edit a staff-authored message, announcement, or application decision draft. Action records may include the requester and target IDs, action, outcome, date, and the original and edited text of a staff message.
  • Automatic staff-command enforcement: in the Potential Millionaires Discord server, a fixed command check may apply a one-hour Discord timeout when someone without the required staff role directly asks the bot to run a staff-only action. The check uses the request text and Discord roles or permissions. The bot records the member, request ID, outcome, and time for review. You can contact staff to dispute a timeout. Staff review report evidence and decide penalties in individual reports.

3. Why we use information

Contractual necessity / service delivery (GDPR Article 6(1)(b), where applicable): we use the information needed to provide the bot commands, dashboard access, sign-in, support, and other features you request. Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f), where applicable): we use relevant information to protect servers against raids, prevent abuse, secure the tools, and keep security and action logs, while considering people’s rights and interests. Consent (Article 6(1)(a), where applicable): we rely on consent for optional saved browser preferences where required. We may also process information when necessary to meet a legal obligation.

4. Cookies and similar browser storage

Our current site code does not use advertising or analytics cookies. It does use browser local storage and session storage for the privacy-choice control and dashboard features. Cloudflare Turnstile is shown at staff/ER:LC dashboard entry, sign-in, and moderator-code steps; its challenge may use browser storage needed to prevent abuse. Belgian privacy guidance treats local storage and similar technologies as cookie-like trackers for applicable rules, so we describe them in our Cookies and browser storage policy.

The cookie-choice preference is kept in local storage for up to 180 days so we can remember your selection. The dashboard session token, recent activity, and temporary settings are kept in session storage. The dashboard backend address is kept in session storage by default; it is saved in local storage only if you choose “Accept preferences.” You can change this choice using “Cookie choices” in the footer or clear browser storage in your browser settings.

When you leave our site to sign in with Discord or use another provider, that provider may use its own cookies or similar technologies on its service. Those providers control their own storage and privacy choices.

5. Service providers and other recipients

  • GitHub Pages: hosts the public website and may process technical request information for delivery and security.
  • Discord: provides the bot, community, ticket and archive channels, and OAuth sign-in. Ticket transcripts and audit posts are stored in Discord channels configured by the server owner.
  • Cloudflare Workers and D1: host the ER:LC dashboard API and its database, including hashed dashboard/session access records and the dashboard’s encrypted ER:LC key.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: checks staff/ER:LC dashboard entry, dashboard sign-in, and moderator-code submissions for automated abuse. It receives the client-side signals described above. It is not used for advertising or analytics.
  • Discord bot database and hosting: the current bot code uses a local SQLite database and local environment file on the machine where the bot runs. The current project configuration does not identify a separate third-party bot-hosting company. If the bot is moved to an external host, that provider may process the data stored on its machine and this notice must be updated.
  • Roblox and ER:LC API: receive the requests needed for Roblox experiences and authorized ER:LC server lookups and commands.
  • OpenAI: provides AI replies, public web lookup, and staff-request interpretation through its API. It receives the text or selected media described above when you use those features. Older versions of the bot may use Groq for AI replies until upgraded.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy notices. We do not sell personal information.

Some providers may process information outside the European Economic Area. Their own privacy notices explain their locations and transfer arrangements. Email potentialmillionairesofficial@gmail.com or contact us through the support server if you need information about a specific feature or provider.

6. How long information is kept

  • Dashboard sessions stop working at sign-out or after six hours. The browser session token is cleared when you sign out or the browser session ends. Cloudflare D1 stores only a hash; sign-out deletes that record, and an expired record is deleted when that session is next checked.
  • One-time Discord sign-in state is valid for 10 minutes. Moderator join-code hashes expire after seven days unless revoked earlier. Pending moderator requests remain until the owner approves or denies them. Approved moderator records remain until access is revoked or the record is removed.
  • The dashboard’s encrypted ER:LC API key remains in Cloudflare D1 until replaced or deleted. The bot’s ER:LC key remains in the local environment file until replaced or removed by the bot operator.
  • We do not keep Turnstile challenge tokens or its browser signals in our database. Cloudflare sets its own retention for signals received while checking a challenge; see the Turnstile Privacy Addendum linked above.
  • ER:LC action audit messages posted to Discord remain under that Discord server’s retention and deletion controls. The dashboard keeps up to eight recent activity entries in session storage until they are replaced, cleared, or the browser session ends.
  • The bot’s SQLite database retains configuration, case records, in-progress application data, level and shift records, and role settings until the bot operator deletes or resets them. In-progress application answers are cleared from the database when an application is submitted or cancelled; submitted application content may remain in the server’s staff archive.
  • Ticket transcripts, moderation/anti-raid records, and action audit messages may remain in Discord channels or archives until authorized server staff delete them. Leaving the Discord server does not itself delete those records. We will review verified data-rights requests and erase records we control when required by law; legal obligations or other permitted grounds may require some records to be kept.
  • The bot’s rate-limit attempt counters use 10-minute time buckets. Discord, GitHub, and Cloudflare control retention of their own technical and security logs.
  • The bot disables saved responses in the OpenAI API. OpenAI may still retain abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days by default, with possible exceptions described in its API data controls.
  • Hosting-provider security logs are controlled by the relevant provider.

7. Your choices and rights

Where the GDPR applies, you may request access to your personal data, correction of inaccurate data, erasure, restriction of processing, or portability. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time; this does not affect earlier processing. To make a request about information we control, email potentialmillionairesofficial@gmail.com or use the Discord support link above and open a private ticket; identify the feature and Discord account involved. We may need to verify the request before acting and will respond under applicable law. You can also sign out of the dashboard and revoke Discord authorization in your Discord settings. Requests cannot remove information controlled by Discord, Roblox, or another provider.

You may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, including the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / APD-GBA). The GDPR text describes these rights.

8. Children and age rules

Roblox experiences may be accessible to younger players and are governed by Roblox’s age settings and privacy rules. Our Discord bot, community features, and dashboard require users to meet Discord’s minimum age in their country. We do not ask children to provide email addresses, phone numbers, or home addresses through this website. If you believe a child has sent personal information directly to one of our support or dashboard features, email potentialmillionairesofficial@gmail.com or contact us through the support server so we can review the request and take appropriate action.

9. Security and updates

We use access checks and keep private credentials in server-side configuration rather than public website files. No online service can guarantee perfect security. We may update this notice as features or providers change and will revise the date at the top of this page.

10. External privacy notices

See the notices for Discord, Cloudflare and its Turnstile Privacy Addendum, GitHub, Roblox, OpenAI’s API data controls, and Groq for older bot versions.

Browser storage choices are explained in our Cookies and browser storage policy. This notice must be updated if the actual providers, features, or retention practices change.
