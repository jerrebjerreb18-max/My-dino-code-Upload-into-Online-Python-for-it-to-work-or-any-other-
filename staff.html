Join the staff team

Help players, keep the community welcoming, or work behind the scenes on games and events.

How to apply

  1. Open the applications channel in our Discord and select Apply for staff.
  2. Choose a team and keep your DMs open to answer its questions.
  3. Send SUBMIT when you’re done, or CANCEL to stop.

Off-platform roles are 16+. Staff review applications privately.
