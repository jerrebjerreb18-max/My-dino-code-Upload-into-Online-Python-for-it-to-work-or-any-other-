Guides and commands

Use slash commands in Discord. Availability depends on your server and role. The status checks, support links, and staff tools below require the CommunityDesk Support Update.

Open a private ticket

Guide link

Find the Support panel in our Discord and choose the category that fits. Explain what happened and what you tried before you contacted us.

Keep login details, tokens, and other secrets out of your ticket.

Manage AI replies in a ticket

Guide link

Use Toggle AI replies in an eligible support ticket to turn automated OpenAI replies on or off. Staff can help with AI replies off.

Read the Privacy Policy for how ticket text is handled.

Get ready to apply for staff

Guide link

Applications start in our Discord. Review the staff teams and eligibility, then select Apply for staff in the applications channel.

  • Review the team’s duties and requirements.
  • Allow DMs from the server so the bot can send its questions.
  • Prepare a short example of how you’ve helped a community or handled a difficult situation.

Check your staff application

Guide link

Manage the ticket queue

Guide link
/ticketops queue

Ticket staff can filter open tickets by assignment or priority. Use /ticketops workload for team counts or /ticketops status for one ticket.

Use /ticketops assign, /ticketops release, and /ticketops priority to organize work. Save a private handoff with /ticketops note and read it with /ticketops notes.

Review backlogs and setup

Guide link
/desk reports

Potential Millionaires staff can review waiting reports and use /desk applications for the application backlog. /desk health checks the support channel, staff role, and private archive.

Review closed-ticket cleanup

Guide link
/closed-tickets

In Potential Millionaires, this previews eligible closed tickets for members with The Potentials role or Discord Administrator permission. Set prepare to True to request confirmation, or select the bot’s Discord mention and send @Millionaire Bot delete all closed tickets.

Review the list before selecting Delete listed tickets. Only the requester can confirm. The bot checks that each channel is a closed ticket with a verified archived transcript.
